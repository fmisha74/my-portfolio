About Me

My Journey

Ever since I was a kid, I've had a deep passion for technology and everything tech-related, though I didn't always have access to the latest gadgets like my peers. In high school, I decided to take a coding class and join Girls Who Code at my school—and that decision changed everything. I discovered that I didn't need the fanciest hardware to create something amazing; all I needed was curiosity and a computer. That spark led me to major in Computer Science, and I graduated in May 2025 with a degree and a drive to build secure, impactful technology. Now, as a backend developer specializing in cybersecurity, I focus on creating systems that are not only powerful but also protected—because I believe everyone deserves access to safe, reliable technology.
